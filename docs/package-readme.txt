AH Quick Shots
==============

Makes videos of the good moments in your Aces High III replays. It is a page
in your web browser that drives the game's Film Viewer for you: Film Viewer
draws the picture and renders the video with the replay's sound.

Unofficial: not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with HiTech Creations.


STARTING
--------

1. Start Film Viewer: start Aces High and in its main menu choose
   Editing Tools, then Film Viewer. The game closes and Film Viewer opens.
2. Double-click "AH Quick Shots" on your desktop. The first start puts that
   shortcut there; until then, double-click "AH Quick Shots.bat" in this
   folder. The page opens in your browser and connects by itself.

If Windows asks whether to run a file from the internet, choose Run: the
download is free and not paid-signed. To skip the question next time,
right-click the downloaded zip, choose Properties, tick Unblock, press OK,
and unzip it again.


MAKING A VIDEO
--------------

The page asks one thing at a time, in four steps shown at its top. The
navy button at the bottom right goes on; the button at the bottom left goes
back.

1. Replay: click a replay. The newest come first.
2. Moment: click one of your fights, for example "You shot down Lefty59",
   then press Film this moment. Everything lists all that happened in the
   replay; Any place lets you film any part of it.
3. Camera: Observer flies beside you and films the fight, Tripod stands
   still and follows, Pilot's view is what you saw from the cockpit.
   Left, Right, Higher, Lower, Closer and Farther move the camera; - 2 s
   and + 2 s make the shot longer or shorter. Watch it plays the shot in
   Film Viewer's window, with sound. Then press Use this camera.
4. Video: choose Quick, Good or Best (each says how long it takes) and
   press Make the video. When it is done it plays on the page, and
   Open folder shows the file.

Your videos are in the folder Videos\AH Quick Shots.
A - and A + at the top of the page make the letters smaller or larger.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

- Keep this folder where its path has no spaces, for example
  C:\ah-quick-shots. Film Viewer cannot write a video to a path with spaces.
- Film Viewer does not run beside the game: close the game first.
- AH Quick Shots has no window of its own. It stops when you sign out of
  Windows, or end "pythonw.exe" in Task Manager.
- Updating: end "pythonw.exe" in Task Manager, then unzip the new version
  over this folder. Your saved shots stay.
- Removing: delete this folder and the desktop shortcut. Nothing else was
  installed or changed.
- A problem? Send the version shown beside the name at the top of the page
  and the file .quickshots\errors.log from this folder.
